Our Mission

Worship That Leads People to Jesus

Maranatha Music Ministries exists to use worship music and the Gospel message to invite people into a relationship with Jesus Christ.

Why We Exist

“To carry the invitation of the Gospel to every listener, through worship that points to Jesus.”

Our Heritage

45 years of Maranatha worship

For more than four decades, Maranatha worship has been sung in churches and homes around the world, with more than 5 million copies sold in 14 languages.

Through that music, tens of thousands of people have made decisions for Christ. The Invitation Series is the next chapter: bringing the same heart for worship and evangelism to a new generation.

What Guides Us

Our commitments

  • 1

    The Gospel First

    Everything we create points to Jesus. The music serves the message, and the message is an invitation to follow Him.

  • 2

    Excellence in Worship

    We create worship with the same care and quality that has marked Maranatha music for generations.

  • 3

    Faithful Stewardship

    We handle every gift with integrity and transparency, so donors can give with confidence.
